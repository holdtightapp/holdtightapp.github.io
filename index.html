Hold Tight: Emergency Plan

Privacy policy and support

Privacy policySupport

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version. Hold Tight does not collect your information. It does not have accounts, advertising or tracking, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

Who we are

The app is published by Jeremy Prendergast. You can contact us at holdtightapp@outlook.com.

What stays on your iPhone

If you use the household plan, pantry, calculators, checklists, reminders, saved items or contacts, the details you enter are stored only on your iPhone. They can include names, ages, notes, food stock, items you add to checklists, and contact names and numbers. We do not receive them, and we cannot see them.

Contacts

If you add someone from your contacts, the app uses Apple's contact picker. Only the name and the number you choose are copied into the app. The app does not read the rest of your contacts.

Reminders and notifications

If you switch on a reminder, the app asks permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone only. No internet connection or server is used.

Torch

The torch button uses your iPhone's torch. The app does not take photos or use the camera for anything else.

Printing, sharing and saving

When you choose Print, Share or Save to Files, the document goes where you send it. We do not receive a copy.

Links to other websites and phone numbers

Some screens link to other organisations, such as the British Red Cross, or let you tap a phone number. When you open a link, that organisation's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

No tracking, analytics or ads

The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not share your information with anyone.

Backups

Your iPhone may include the app's data in your own device backups, for example iCloud backup, if you have those switched on. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your information

You can edit or delete anything you have entered inside the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its data from the phone.

If you email us

If you write to holdtightapp@outlook.com, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you, and keep them only as long as needed.

Children

The app is not designed for children and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Support

For help or questions about the app, email holdtightapp@outlook.com.

Hold Tight gives general preparedness information based on UK official sources. It is not a substitute for advice from the emergency services. In an emergency, call 999.
